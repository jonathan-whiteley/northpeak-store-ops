HERO QUESTION
=============
"Store 214 (Denver) is out of the Summit Down Parka in the middle of a cold snap.
 Why is it out, what's the best recovery move, and can you make it happen?"

The app answers this as a DECISION, not a lookup: it surfaces the shortfall
(Visualize), prescribes the ranked recovery move and drafts the memo (Assist),
and commits the approved transfer back to Postgres with a human in the loop
(Act) — then the committed decision shows up on the next read (closed loop).

Hero entity: STORE-0214 x SKU-APP-04412 (Summit Down Parka)
