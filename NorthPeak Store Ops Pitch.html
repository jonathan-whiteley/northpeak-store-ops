The problem · Dana Ruiz, SVP Retail Operations
One cold snap flipped demand: the same five coats are sold out in the North and piling toward markdown in the South.
Inventory was allocated to a normal-weather plan. A cold snap three weeks ago pulled demand North faster than the batch replenishment cycle could react. Today Dana sees two colors on one map: red stockouts in the North, amber dead stock in the South.
The question she asks
"Store 214 is short on the Summit Down Parka. What's the best recovery move?"
Store 214 · Denver, CO · today it takes weeks to answer

~$4.8M

The outcome we drive
From "find out weeks later" to "see it, get the move, act today."

The platform ranks the best recovery move for each short store: transfer from a nearby store, expedite from the warehouse, or offer a similar item, each scored by recaptured revenue.
